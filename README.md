I am a Product Engineer and Software Architect with **12+ years of experience** specializing in high-performance SaaS platforms, enterprise school ERP & LMS systems, custom mobile applications, and end-to-end business automation. 

Every platform I build is designed for long-term reliability—focusing on clean data models, secure APIs, and interfaces that teams can operate without heavy engineering support.
